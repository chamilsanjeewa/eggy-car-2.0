How to Play

Drive as far as you can without dropping the egg.

Controls

→ / DGas
← / ABrake, then reverse
PPause
RRestart
CChange camera
NNavigation on/off
MMute

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen pedals.

🧴 Glue bottle

Glues the egg into your vehicle for 15 seconds. Usually found above a jump ramp — you'll need speed.

⛽ Fuel can

Refills your tank. Running dry ends the run once you roll to a stop.

❤️ Extra life

Saves your egg once if it cracks and puts it back in the vehicle.

💰 Big coin

Worth 10 coins, floating high above hill crests and ramps.

Tips

  • Ease off before the top of a hill so the egg doesn't fly out on the way down.
  • In the air, gas tips the nose up and brake tips it down — land on both wheels.
  • Spend coins on Suspension upgrades early; they make every vehicle gentler on the egg.
