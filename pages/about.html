About Eggy Car

A physics driving game about getting one very fragile egg as far as possible.

The idea

You drive over endless hills with an egg rolling around in the back. Too much speed and it bounces out; too little and you won't clear the next hill. Every run is a balance between going fast and keeping the egg in one piece.
